Last updated: October 1, 2026

Smart Plan Savings (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with our website and services.

We operate a website that helps homeowners connect with licensed service professionals for home-related needs. By using our site or contacting us, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

If you choose to contact us by phone or otherwise communicate with us, you may share information such as your name, phone number, address, or details about the service you need. We use this information only to respond to your request and to provide or coordinate the services you asked for.

3. How We Use Information

We use information you provide to:

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted service partners who help fulfill your request, and with service providers who support our operations under appropriate confidentiality terms. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

9. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information we hold about you, subject to applicable law. California residents have additional rights described in our CCPA notice.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of our site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the phone number listed on our website.
