California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Last updated: October 1, 2025

This notice supplements our Privacy Policy and applies to California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

Categories of Personal Information

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect identifiers (such as contact details you provide), commercial information related to services requested, and limited internet activity information needed to operate our website.

We do not collect sensitive personal information beyond what is reasonably necessary to provide our services.

Purposes of Collection

We use this information to connect you with service providers, improve our site, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration in the conventional sense. Certain disclosures to service partners or advertising/analytics partners may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under CCPA. California residents may opt out of such sale/sharing via our Do Not Sell / Opt-Out form.

Your Rights

  • Right to Know — Request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete — Request deletion of personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to Correct — Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing — Direct us not to sell or share your personal information.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information — Where applicable.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

How to Exercise Your Rights

Submit a request through our Opt-Out / Privacy Request form or by calling the number on this site. We will verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

Authorized Agents

You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. We may require proof of authorization and verification of your identity.

Contact

For CCPA-related inquiries, use the opt-out form or the contact methods listed on this site.
